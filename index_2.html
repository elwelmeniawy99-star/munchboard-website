    Good to know

    How far ahead should I order?

    We suggest 48 hours for boxes and 72 hours for larger trays. Ask us about last-minute orders.

    Can you handle allergies and diets?

    Tell us in your notes. We offer vegetarian options and can talk through other needs, but we can't guarantee an allergen-free kitchen.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, within our local area for a delivery fee. Pickup is always free.
